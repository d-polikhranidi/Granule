# Privacy Policy

Granule, for the Mac, iPad and iPhone, collects no data about you.

- **Nothing is collected or sent to us.** The app has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no
  accounts. It doesn't talk to any server of ours: there is none.
- **It talks only to the servers you add.** Your queries and their results go between your
  device and your ClickHouse servers (through an SSH server if you set one up), and nowhere else.
  The public playground is ClickHouse's own server, play.clickhouse.com, used only when you choose
  to try it.
- **What you set up stays on your device.** Connections, query history, saved queries and
  settings are kept on your device. Passwords, SSH keys and client certificates are kept in the
  Keychain. Deleting a connection deletes what was kept for it.
- **Files** are read or written only when you choose them: a clickhouse-client configuration to
  import, a key or certificate, or an export.

Your ClickHouse servers, and the networks between you and them, are run by you or your
organization, under your own policies.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/d-polikhranidi/Granule/issues.
